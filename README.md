# Customer Engagement & Product Utilization Analytics for Retention Strategy

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer retention using engagement behavior, product utilization, and relationship strength indicators.

The objective is to identify key drivers of churn and develop retention-focused business strategies for banking institutions.

## Objectives

* Evaluate the relationship between engagement and churn.
* Measure retention impact of product utilization.
* Identify disengaged high-value customers.
* Develop engagement-driven retention strategies.

## Tools Used

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Plotly
* Streamlit

## Key Features

* Customer Engagement Classification
* Product Utilization Analysis
* Relationship Strength Index (RSI)
* At-Risk Premium Customer Detection
* KPI Dashboard
* Interactive Streamlit Application

## KPIs

* Engagement Retention Ratio
* Product Depth Index
* High-Balance Disengagement Rate
* Credit Card Stickiness Score
* Relationship Strength Index

## Deliverables

* Jupyter Notebook Analysis
* Streamlit Dashboard
* Research Paper
* Executive Summary
* KPI Reports

## Key Findings

* Active customers have significantly lower churn rates.
* Multi-product customers demonstrate stronger retention.
* High balances alone do not guarantee customer loyalty.
* Relationship strength is a strong predictor of retention.
